## NEW JERSEY

### Goals

- Support our cohort of four NJ community colleges in growing and sustaining noncredit certificate programs that equip more New Jerseyans to participate in getting, sharing, and discussing reliable local news and information.
- Help project teams solidify pathways for participants to apply their skills and get involved in the local news ecosystem
- Research and identify opportunities to co-develop a community journalism and Documenters program in Morris County
- Establish partnerships with aligned organizations who have similar community-focused programming and better enable other orgs and colleges in NJ to equip people to participate

### Partners

- NJ Council for the Humanities (statewide program partner)
- NJCIC (funder and connector)
- Atlantic Cape Community College, Mercer County Community College, Middlesex College, Sussex County Community College
- Kirby Foundation (funder in Morris County)
- Documenters
- coLAB Arts (partners in Middlesex and Morris County)
