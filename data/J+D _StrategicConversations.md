## NEW MEXICO

### Goals

- Support a new cohort of four NM community colleges in developing and launching programs that equip more New Mexicans to participate in getting, sharing, and discussing reliable local news and information and establish clear, workforce-aligned pathways (internships, entrepreneurship, etc.) for program participants to apply their skills

- Support cohort in planning for 2026-27 programs
  ● Context/History
  2024: NMLNF and J+D teamed up in the spring and co-led a multi-month listening and asset mapping period in which we connected 1:1 with college and community contacts recommended by NMLNF; gathered and synthesized their insights and feedback about the local news and community college landscapes in NM; and designed a draft grant program based upon that feedback.

  2024-25: NMLNF and J+D jointly recruited a seven-person advisory board to participate in an iterative feedback cycle, in which advisory board members reviewed and provided feedback on draft grant program designs, the grant application and review process, and the grant application itself.

  The J+D team members also analyzed the strengths/challenges of the NJ cohort grant application process and incorporated learnings from that program during the NM grant program design.

  2025: launched an open call for grant applications for NM community colleges to design programs to equip more New Mexicans to participate in getting, sharing, and discussing reliable local news and information and to establish clear pathways (internships, entrepreneurship, etc.) for program participants to apply their skills. Applications were read and reviewed by the advisory board; J+D and NMLNF jointly conducted phone and Zoom follow-up conversations with individual college teams as needed. Four colleges (see partner list below) were selected for the cohort

  General: NMLNF had some staff turnover in 2024-2025 which resulted in diminished staff capacity for the partnership w/J+D, and therefore less support than expected for activities like local outreach and engagement; that has since been remedied with the addition of Denise, their new business development and comms director.

**DESIGN CHALLENGE**

● Goals

- Through the in-person Civic Info Challenge Design Summit, provide the newly-selected cohort of 8 community colleges in the South with the frameworks and thought partnership to turn their program ideas into implementable solutions (with roadmaps)
- Explore how best to partner with the colleges in the cohort and nurture their program ideas, all of which are very different
- Create a process and tools to support individual teams in identifying and securing local funds (philanthropic or otherwise) for their projects, thereby bolstering the programs’ sustainability
- Support colleges that were not accepted into the cohort in developing their ideas

● Context/history

2023: Lumina Foundation first approached the J+D/NJCH team following a panel at the Virginia Local News Summit, to express interest in and support for the community journalism noncredit certificate-focused program that J+D was piloting in NJ

2024: after further cultivation, Lumina awarded J+D a one-year, 100K grant for designing, planning, and executing a Civic Info Challenge for community colleges in the US South, to 1\) learn what community colleges around the region are doing to increase the flow of local news and information in their surrounding communities, and 2\) host an in-person summit to support 5-8 colleges in using design thinking to workshop their ideas.

2025: following a listening period with stakeholders from around the South, J+D developed a call for applications open to individual community college faculty and staff (or teams of CC faculty and staff) in the South. The call launched in spring 2025; we received 11 applications, and a six-person external selection committee selected 8 of those to make up our Challenge cohort. Outreach/recruitment for the program could have been more effective; not sure why we didn’t get more uptake, especially in states where we had connections and did a fair amount of personalized and sustained outreach (e.g., North Carolina, Kentucky, Virginia)

- Current status: all 11 colleges have been notified of their application status. Cohort colleges are finishing up completing and submitting an information form to J+D, to gather key details for the Challenge summit and program launch. Challenge summit planned for November 6-8; summit program/invites currently in development w/J+D team.

  - Cohort colleges: each college team to receive seed funds of $5k (to be co-structured with J+D) \> design and develop their ideas \> pursue more seed funding to continue the work \+ a local funder \> J+D works as a consultant in a structured way
  - Colleges not selected for the cohort: offered the option for free consulting time with J+D in early 2026, to support them in continuing to think about their project. Will be following up with interested colleges to offer some specific ways that they can work together w/J+D to develop their ideas and seek additional resources (including funding) for making their projects happen.
